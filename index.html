Kenya's Education Administration & Management

⚖️ Constitution of 2010 · Basic Education Act · CBC Transition (2026)

Introduction & Legal Framework

  • Constitution of 2010 — supreme law, establishes fundamental rights & devolution
  • Basic Education Act — legal framework for basic education management
  • Collaborative framework — each stakeholder has a specific legal, administrative, or operational role
  • Stakeholders include: TSC, KNUT, KUPPET, Parents, Students

What's new in 2026

  • Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) — now in Senior Secondary phase (Grade 10) as of January 2026
  • KNUT–KUPPET split — unions publicly disagree on Junior School autonomy
  • Education budget 2026/27 — KSh 784.5 billion, largest sector allocation
  • 39% of Kenyans cite fees/uniforms as biggest Grade 10 transition barrier (Infotrak, Dec 2025–Jan 2026)
"The legal framework remains, but the 2026 reality is defined by CBC implementation and union disagreement."

Teachers Service Commission (TSC)

Established under Article 237 · Independent constitutional commission

FunctionDescription
Human Resource ManagementRegisters, recruits, deploys, transfers, promotes, and disciplines teachers in all public primary, junior secondary, and senior secondary schools.
Quality Assurance & StandardsMonitors teacher performance and compliance with professional teaching standards across schools.
Capacity BuildingCoordinates Continuous Professional Development (CPD) programs to equip educators for curriculum reforms, such as the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC).
Policy AdvisoryAdvises the national government on teacher demand, supply, and overall budget implications.

2026 Updates

  • Recruitment shift: No new primary teacher recruitment — all 16,000 new hires target Junior and Senior Schools
  • 20,000 intern teachers to be converted to permanent and pensionable terms
  • KSh 422.95 billion proposed 2026/27 budget (up from KSh 387.2B)
  • KSh 2 billion for promotions across all levels
  • 70,000 teachers annually to be trained on CBC
  • New JSS administrative structure proposed — Heads of Institutions and Deputies, facing union resistance
"TSC's core mandate remains, but its recruitment and training focus has decisively shifted to Junior and Senior Secondary Schools."

Kenya National Union of Teachers (KNUT)

Represents: Teachers in public primary schools and entry-level positions

RoleDescription
Collective BargainingNegotiates Collective Bargaining Agreements (CBAs) with the TSC regarding salaries, medical cover, allowances, and retirement terms.
Welfare & Rights AdvocacyProtects teachers against unfair disciplinary actions, arbitrary transfers, or poor working conditions.
Instructional LeadershipContributes to national educational dialogues and advocates for adequate resources so teachers can deliver quality education.

2026 Updates

  • Opposes JSS autonomy — supports comprehensive school model (JSS within primary schools)
  • SG Collins Oyuu: "Grades 7, 8, and 9 are successfully domiciled in primary schools. Do not bring in what was not part of the reforms."
  • Warns that separating JSS would disrupt learning and waste limited budget
  • Publicly clashes with KUPPET on this issue — National Assembly Education Committee has documented the disagreement
  • Advocates for comprehensive school under one headteacher and one BOM
"KNUT's position: Keep Junior Schools within primary schools — separation would harm learners and waste resources."

Kenya Union of Post Primary Education Teachers (KUPPET)

Represents: Secondary schools, Junior Secondary Schools (JSS), TVETs, and tertiary colleges

RoleDescription
Targeted RepresentationNegotiates specialised terms of service unique to post-primary institutions, such as laboratory allowances and risk management frameworks.
Professional Development AdvocacyPushes the government and TSC to offer sponsorship, fair promotions, and adequate training for post-primary tutors during systemic shifts like the CBC rollout.
Legal SupportOffers legal defense and representations for member teachers facing employment or administrative disputes.

2026 Updates

  • Demands JSS autonomy — wants separate principals, deputy principals, BOM, and funding for Junior Schools
  • SG Akello Misori calls domiciling JSS in primary schools "a mistake"
  • Argues: "A supervisor must qualify to do whatever they manage" — P1 or ECDE holders should not supervise degree-level JSS teachers
  • Wants JSS relocated to secondary schools where labs and facilities already exist
  • At Basic Education Bill 2026 hearings: "Anywhere they have talked about comprehensive school should entirely be deleted"
  • Publicly clashes with KNUT — documented by National Assembly Education Committee
"KUPPET's position: Junior Schools must be autonomous — the current arrangement undermines qualifications and resource access."

Parents & Students

👪 Parents

Engage through PTA and BOM

  • Financial & Resource Support: Co-fund infrastructure, buy uniforms, supply learning materials, pay approved levies.
  • Governance & Co-Management: Elect BOM representatives, participate in strategic planning, budgeting, and oversight.
  • Learning Facilitation: Monitor homework, foster discipline, reinforce values at home.

2026 Updates

  • Five legally mandated BOM committees (Basic Education Act, Sec. 61):
    • Finance, Procurement & General Purposes
    • Academic Standards, Quality & Environment
    • Discipline, Ethics & Integrity
    • Audit
    • Human Rights & Student Welfare
  • 39% of Kenyans cite high fees and uniform costs as biggest Grade 10 transition barrier (Infotrak, n=1,000)
  • 19% say they lacked adequate information about CBC
  • 45% of parents dissatisfied with CBC grading — "don't see a difference" from 8-4-4

🎓 Students

Primary internal stakeholders

  • Active Learning Engagement: Accountable for academic performance, completing assignments, respecting school rules.
  • Participatory Leadership: Elect student council leaders (prefects) as a bridge between students and administration.
  • Feedback Mechanism: Provide vital data on teacher attendance, facility quality, safety, and learning environment.

2026 Updates

  • Continuous assessment replaces one-off exams: EE (Exceeding) / ME (Meeting) / AE (Approaching) / BE (Below Expectations)
  • 45% of Kenyans dissatisfied with CBC grading — parents "don't see a difference" from 8-4-4
  • PS Julius Bitok: parents are "trying to interpret KJSEA using the old KCPE lens"
  • Pathway selection challenges: Students request changes shortly after placement — often due to peer influence or parental pressure, not genuine interest
  • Project-based learning: Students complete authentic projects addressing community problems
  • ICT competency required: Mobile devices used for research, content creation, interactive learning
"Parents and students are not passive recipients — they are active participants. But CBC has placed new financial and information burdens on families."
